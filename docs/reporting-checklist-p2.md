# P2 reporting checklist and omissions

This crosswalk uses [CHEERS 2022](https://www.ispor.org/heor-resources/good-practices/cheers)
as a reporting aid, not as evidence that the draft model is methodologically
validated or publishable.

| Reporting dimension | Current artifact | Open for CHHHS / study authors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population and setting | `protocol-p2.md` §1; versioned eligibility | Actual reach, adult vs paediatric boundary, rural transfers and disposition checks |
| Comparators | Two distinct strategies; existing care NOT presumed absent | Existing and proposed rosters, activities, actual overlap and implementation fidelity |
| Perspective | CHHHS provider resources vs separate budget impact | Finance agreement, cash realisability and other costs shifted outside CHHHS |
| Time horizon/discounting | 12-month programme, inpatient follow-up, no discount within one year | FY-tail allocation, longer horizon and discount policy if selected |
| Health outcomes | Operational + safety endpoints with exact clocks | Outcome occurrence, treatment limitations, ward harm, patient experience and utility if sought |
| Resources and valuation | Three segment Dapane valuation boundaries | Observed consumption, price year, costs, offsets and local demand/capacity |
| Uncertainty | P1 illustrative independent triangular draw engine | Source-backed joint uncertainty, correlations, structural and parameter calibration |
| Structural model | Cohort expected-cost resource scenario | Causal estimand, censoring, time-varying throughput and optional DES after evidence |
| Equity and engagement | Prespecified review axes; no subgroup results | Governance, consumer/staff consultation, feasibility and disclosure safeguards |
| Publication/readiness | P2 CLI admission receipt | Clinical approval, provider qualification, external validation and reproducibility |

A favourable economic balance **must never override unresolved safety**. A
`P(net resource benefit >= 0)` calculated from unvalidated assumptions is not a
probability that the CHHHS business case will save money.
