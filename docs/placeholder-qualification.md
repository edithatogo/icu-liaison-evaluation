The placeholder register is a **work queue, not a data source or approval**. Its
JSON pointers bind each obligation to its current unvalidated/synthetic/public
source-context field, identify the prospective custodian and required evidence,
and name the downstream decision gates. This deliberately allows interface
implementation and deterministic validation to proceed without a claim that
Cairns and Hinterland Hospital and Health Service (CHHHS) has supplied data.

## Run the current synthetic programme

From the root of the source checkout, use the repository environment from the
[README setup](../README.md#tested-basic-workflow).

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m icu_liaison.placeholders_cli audit
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m icu_liaison.placeholders_cli dry-run \
  --output-dir /absolute/path/to/a/new/synthetic-output-directory
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m icu_liaison.placeholders_cli audit --require-site-ready
```

The first command validates **all 33 source bindings** and prints the open
external gates; the second generates an atomic, hash-inventoried P3 quality
receipt, P4/P5 economic report and P6 trace/holdout results. The third exits
with status **2** because placeholder-led development is not clinical or
investment readiness. All outputs are **synthetic**, even if their calculations
are internally consistent. Existing core modelling, SciPy and Kairos contracts
are reused; this layer contains no new scheduler, VOI solver or valuation core.

The release archive **excludes all observed local data**, including original
patient identifiers, hospitalisation keys, linkable timestamp extracts,
financial ledger exports and recorded governance decisions. P3 synthetic CSV
fixtures reside under `model-inputs/placeholders/p3-synthetic/` and have
precommitted SHA-256 checksums. Never commit any real replacements in their place.

## What each replacement requires

`model-inputs/placeholders/registry.json` is the controlling queue. Each row has:

- `binding.artifact` and `binding.pointer`: exact current source-field binding,
  including a `source_class` of `synthetic`, `missing`, or
  `unverified_public_metadata`;
- `owner_role` and `prospective_source`: the party and likely system to query;
- `replacement.requirements`: inspectable source or study-design acceptance;
- `blocks`: gates that remain open until externally qualified.

`replacement-checklist.csv`, generated by `dry-run`, is a readable per-item
handoff. Synthetic parameter choices in P4 remain within
`model-inputs/p4/illustrative.json`, not duplicated or overwritten by P7.
The publisher receipt confirms the 2024/25 ANZICS CCR 20.4% figure and its
page locator. The figure remains a pooled public proxy. Independent review
of its intended use and estimation of the local adult index-ward-discharge
rate are still due.
[Publisher check](../verification/p7-anzics-2024-25-publisher-2026-11-24.json)

The most material obligations are: local ICU episode/72-hour follow-up and
missingness; existing and proposed roster/FTE; causes of discharge delay;
intervention effects from a credible counterfactual; marginal ICU/ward and
return-event costs; staffed ICU and ward calendars and rejected/censored ICU
requests; clinical harms and appropriate rescue; cultural/data-governance,
clinical, independent health-economic and Finance approvals; publisher-document
verification; native Kairos/Dapane/Voiage provider receipts; and dedicated
repository hosted CI.

## Introducing real evidence (future; site-private only)

**Do not turn `status: "placeholder"` into `"verified"`.** Editing a source
prior to claim local empirical status causes the public synthetic audit to
fail closed. Site-specific inputs must be developed through an authorised
private-data lane with an explicit approval and retention plan.

1. Obtain the necessary clinical, information-governance, consumer and First
   Nations data-governance authorisations as applicable. Confirm extract
   pseudonymisation and secure processing environment before importing.
2. Prepare source-specific manifests, mappings and completeness reports using
   the P3 `icu_liaison.p3_cli` controlled local intake. Preserve observed
   censoring, source timestamps, denominator and incident-classification caveats.
3. For each replacement, create a **private** candidate evidence file and a
   candidate receipt, e.g. the documented schema
   `model-inputs/placeholders/replacement-candidates.schema.json`.
   Store both in approved private storage outside the checkout or in its
   ignored `private/` or `.local/` directories.
   Call `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m icu_liaison.placeholders_cli review-candidates
   --submission /private/absolute/path/to/submission.json`.
   The command checks the location, ID/type and byte hash. The custodian
   confirms storage access, and independent reviewers assess source authority
   and scientific adequacy.
4. Have independent clinicians, economists, Finance, data custodians and
   governance officers adjudicate relevant source and causal evidence. Introduce
   site-calibrated scenarios in **private** storage, with separate model version,
   qualified distribution inputs and accepted outcome definitions. Maintain
   reproducible derived aggregate reports and receipts.
5. Execute and qualify the separately pinned Dapane, Voiage and Kairos
   providers in supported environments with reviewed binaries and exact
   dependency closures; do not substitute P7 placeholders for native runs.
6. Only a separate, independently approved site-release workflow could ever
   produce an investment-ready status. **No public P7 command can do so.**

## Technical and interpretative limitations

The P3 synthetic example contains only a few fictional episodes. Its aggregate
counts are **not** used to recalibrate the P4 illustrative annual cohort of
1,000. The P6 one-bed, four-hour trace is a manually specified fixture; it is
**not** a Kairos-run simulation. P6 holdout observations are a synthetic,
admitted-only exercise, not complete demand including censoring and decline.
The P4/P5 output remains a sensitivity analysis of hypothetical priors. All
observed effect estimates, causal validity, provider validation and real
clinical safety acceptance remain open.
