# ADR-0008 — Canonical metrics and fail-closed scientific claims

**Decision:** Accepted for *source architecture* on 19 November 2026, not clinical, Finance or provider qualification.

Adopt P10 as the common measurement layer beneath P7 empirical admission and P9 presentation: stable denominator/metric IDs, data-source links, versioned uncertainty classes, proposed provenance and visual review controls, and a claim policy which defaults to denial unless evidence is independently verified by a trusted authority.

**Consequences:** P9 catalogue revision v3 adds required M-ID and uncertainty-class references. Previous v2 and schema are archived. No numerical code in P4/P5 is replaced. Missing site data and approvals remain absent, not presumed compliant.

**Rejected:** manual `approved: true` Boolean, undifferentiated readmissions, unknown-as-zero, NWAU-as-cash, visual assets without source digests, and synthetic effects presented as causal.
