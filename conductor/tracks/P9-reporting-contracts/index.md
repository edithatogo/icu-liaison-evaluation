# P9 reporting contract

[Clinical Director request](../../../docs/clinical-director-data-request.md) | [Figure contract](../../../contracts/reporting/catalogue.json) | [Contract schema](../../../contracts/reporting/catalogue.schema.json) | [Granular tasks](figure-workplan.md) | [Design decisions](design.md) | [Plan](plan.md)

The catalogue contains seven tables and 24 figures. P11 renders 14 figures
for each fictional scenario. F01 compares strategies and 13 P9 figures have
an accessible index, source CSVs and independent SVG mark checks. F05–F09
retain the original external PNG references alongside their rebuilt source
figures. Ten figure contracts await governed data or qualified provider outputs.

The [run coverage check](../../../contracts/reporting/README.md#check-a-run)
reports which of the seven tables and 24 figures a P11 run produced, with
artifact hashes and the evidence needed for blocked outputs. The private
presentation has its own [figure profile](../../../contracts/reporting/evaluation-deck.json).

P9 consumes the shared [P10 measurement/evidence assurance](../P10-measurement-figure-assurance/index.md) contracts. P10 owns metric meanings and claims; P9 retains figure generation and P5 independent numerical review.
