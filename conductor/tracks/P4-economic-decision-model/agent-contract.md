assumptions and export contracts only. It does not own Dapane, Voiage,
ANZICS, CHHHS local databases or CareOps Sim shared engineering. It may
edit `src/icu_liaison/p4_*.py`, versioned illustrative inputs, P4 docs,
track and tests. Reuse P3 source-catalogue validation through an independent
pure function rather than a competing registry validator.
