# P4 risks

- The pooled all-age binational ANZICS rate has limited applicability to
  Cairns adult ICU care. The retained 2024/25 PDF matches the publisher
  receipt. Independent admission for the intended use remains open.
- Confounding: no causal effect or censoring-aware ward-return analysis from
  current inputs; relative effect is an illustrative parameter.
- Accounting: discharge-ready time, subsequent returns, and staffing changes
  are distinct cost components. Financial and opportunity values must not be
  summed with the same recovered ICU day again.
- Safety: excess admissions can reflect appropriate rescue; hospital/ward
  deaths, deterioration, treatment goals and ward staffing remain unmeasured.
- Distribution: Gaussian copula and independent marginals are declared
  judgemental assumptions, not posterior calibration or confidence intervals.
- Hosted checks cover synthetic runs. Scoped native provider evidence is
  recorded in P1. Broader provider admission and site clinical and Finance
  approval remain open.
