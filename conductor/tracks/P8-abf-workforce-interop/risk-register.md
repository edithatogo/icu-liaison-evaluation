# P8 material risks and stops

1. **Funding substitution:** NWAU/NEP and actual allocated budget are different;
   MCHS must not create a hospital revenue claim without Finance evidence.
2. **ICU hourly funding double count:** AICU and bundled/removed LOS must follow
   the selected year's official formula, not a universal hourly multiplier.
3. **Year and coding mismatch:** A 2025 official calculator and a 2026 equation
   are not interchangeable; no retroactive regrouping or source-year leakage.
4. **Nursing misclassification:** HP3 published rates are not nursing rates;
   level, award and eligibility require source/HR confirmation.
5. **Labour component overlap:** Super, leave loadings, penalties, backfill and
   productive-hour conversion must not be double counted or silently omitted.
6. **Economic versus cash:** Released ICU bed-days may be usable capacity but
   not immediately avoidable cash; Dapane keeps basis and price-year explicit.
7. **False integration evidence:** Schema/fixtures and exact source commits do
   not establish installed binaries, real billing, local roster or clinical effect.
8. **Source rights/security:** Do not commit licensed annual weights, private
   payroll/HR rules, patient episodes or CHHHS transaction data to public Git.

A failing official calculator fixture, unverified Nursing Award, unavailable
provider binary or missing local funding rule blocks the affected track; it
must not trigger a custom fallback that purports to compute the same number.
