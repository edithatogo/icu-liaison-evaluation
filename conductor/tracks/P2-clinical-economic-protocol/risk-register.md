# Risks and controls

- **Denominator drift:** CCR live ward/home delay and return episodes differ
  from the P2 first-index adult ward cohort; pin the local numerator/denominator
  and use public rates only as external proxies.
- **Survivor/ascertainment bias:** death, transfer, unknown return intention
  and short follow-up cannot all be mapped to no unplanned readmission; P3 needs
  proper risk-set and censoring approach, not only reference booleans.
- **False baseline:** existing liaison/MET activity is unmeasured. A zero baseline
  overstates incremental savings and changes the decision comparator.
- **Unapproved risk tolerance:** avoid invented statistical noninferiority
  margins and missing safety endpoints; block safety conclusions.
- **Double counting:** index ready-to-depart, subsequent return and new service
  costs are nonoverlapping; ward cost offset required; capacity opportunity value
  and budget cash savings are separate outcomes.
- **Case mix and temporality:** transfer policy, staffing, seasonality and
  clinical severity can change independently of the service; observational
  before/after trends are not causal proof.
- **Equity and disclosure:** subgroup data require approved collection,
  appropriate Indigenous governance and minimum safe cell reporting.
- **Source/rights:** CHHHS private extracts, spreadsheets, staff names and
  unpublished case details are not in this source. No local evidence submitted.
