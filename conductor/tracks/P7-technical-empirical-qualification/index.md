# P7 — Qualification and evaluation closeout

[Spec](spec.md) | [Plan](plan.md) | [Verification](test-matrix.md) | [Handoff](handoff.md)

Qualify the existing portable source and its providers without claiming retrospective clinical or economic acceptance. No new generic engine; P0–P6 owners retain their modules and tests. P7 only documents executable verification and external evidence gates.

The [portable qualification command](test-matrix.md#repeat-the-current-qualification)
reconciles the published benchmark receipt and CHHHS request specification.
It checks synthetic P3 denominators and selected P4 accounting rows and
records the source hashes and seed.
