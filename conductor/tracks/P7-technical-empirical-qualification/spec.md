# P7 — Qualification specification

Scope: (1) source-independent P4 accounting oracle; (2) P3 aggregate inference boundaries and data request; (3) 2023/24 primary ANZICS PDF page review and 2024/25 explicit unresolved status; (4) repeatable provider/native qualification checks; (5) CHHHS causal/clinical/Finance and data-governance handoff. Does not authorise direct extraction, native fake execution, service rollout, research publication, or a new GitHub repository.

Required outputs: data-request JSON and generated CSV/MD, source register, independent numerical/QA tests, actual reproducibility receipts and blocker list. P7 cannot mark closure of any provider, site or clinical gate without the relevant real source/binary/approval receipt.

The portable receipt binds the source catalogue, recorded publisher check,
synthetic P3 manifest and feeds, P4 seed and inputs, and the two renderings
of the CHHHS request. The aggregate audit gives the observed-only estimates
and full eligible-cohort ranges under extreme outcomes for unresolved records.
Source admission and the empirical gates require their separate evidence.
