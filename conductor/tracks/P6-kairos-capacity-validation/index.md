[Specification](spec.md) · [Plan](plan.md) · [Test matrix](test-matrix.md) ·

P6.1–P6.2 provide a synthetic neutral trace contract and vectorised capacity
conservation review. This **does not execute Kairos or produce counterfactual
traces**. Actual clinical data, signed approvals, native Kairos interface and
independent replay tests are prerequisites to the optional P6.3–P6.4 work.
