# Additional P10 improvements to prioritise

These are proposals, **not implemented outcomes**.

1. **Decision and patient-safety threshold registry (Must before investment).** Encode clinical adverse-event no-go constraints independently of positive economic break-even.
2. **Transportability and case-mix drift testing (Should).** Stratify by occupancy pressure, shift, rural transfers and staffing regimes before extrapolating synthetic findings.
3. **Expiry, revocation and pricing-year checks (Should).** Make MCHS, Queensland pay sources, HREC/SSA and Finance authority time-bound and invalidate stale outputs.
4. **Decision-directed data collection (Should).** Prioritise missing CHHHS measures by their potential to change a decision, using *qualified* VOI where available.
5. **Adversarial falsification fixtures (Should).** Explicit null/harm effects, missing ready timestamps, competing deaths, inflated cash assumptions, wrong DRG year, mispaired PSA draws.
6. **Prespecified evaluation and implementation fidelity (Must for causal claims).** Freeze analytic estimands, controls and measured liaison contact/dose before site-level causal inference.

Next recommended implementable slice: P10.10 source-to-output provenance digest validation, followed by P10.11 actual responsive/print visual checks.

**P10.14 source-date update:** temporal validity, exclusive expiry, source version and revocation checks now have source-only tests. A trusted external verification/revocation workflow is not implemented and remains a high-priority acceptance gate.

**P10.12 synthetic progress:** five adversarial P4 scenarios now have executable source-only accounting assertions. These are falsification fixtures, not observed harm or structural-model calibration.
