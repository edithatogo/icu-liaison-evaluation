# P10 — Scientific measurement and evidence admission contract

**Owners:** P10 assurance contract owner; P9 renderer/report owner; P7 empirical/governance owner; P5 numerical reviewer. P10 does not certify provider binaries or clinical outcomes.

- D01–D14 define denominator populations, time anchors and missingness. M01–M31 define metric numerators, units, source references and scientific uncertainty.
- T01–T07 and F01–F24 must have linked metric IDs; each figure must share at least one metric with its registered source table.
- No 72-hour death/transfer/incomplete state can be imputed as no-return; no readiness unknown can be counted as zero-hour exit block.
- A figure provenance record requires source/model/seed/renderer/output/independent checks. Well-formed hashes alone **never** prove those checks actually occurred.
- The claim validator can permit the fictional illustration of one verified
  P11 table or figure. The decision binds the source, artifact, measurement
  and policy hashes. Site, Finance, native-provider and investment claims
  require verification by a trusted external authority.
- The visual matrix defines review criteria, and the uncertainty taxonomy
  defines the classes used in the reporting contract. The baseline captures
  are recorded in a separate receipt.
- P4 owns costing, P5 owns arithmetic and P7 owns governance. The v2
  catalogue and schema remain under history. The current P9-v5 catalogue
  links its tables and figures to the registered metrics.
