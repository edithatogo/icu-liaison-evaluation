# P10 — Measurement, provenance and claim assurance

[MoSCoW requirements](requirements.md) | [Specification](spec.md) | [Tasks](plan.md) | [Test matrix](test-matrix.md) | [Future recommendations](additional-recommendations.md)

Defines source-only metric/denominator identities, P9 table/figure links, uncertainty taxonomy, provenance templates, visual acceptance and a fail-closed claim policy.

The source registry contains 31 metrics and 14 denominators. Synthetic
mark checks and responsive and print evidence are available for the 14
implemented figures. The [claim assessment](../../../contracts/reporting/README.md#assess-a-claim)
checks the artifact and binds a fictional illustration to its case, metrics
and denominators. CHHHS data admission, trusted authority verification and
clinical review remain open. [Synthetic verification](../../../verification/portable-synthetic-2026-11-20.json)

[Report edition evidence](../../../contracts/reporting/edition-assurance.md)
records citation extraction, source identity, contextual prose review and
scoped readability. The governed claim and scientific acceptance gates still apply.
