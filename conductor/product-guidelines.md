# Product guidelines

- Label synthetic, external, observed and unverified inputs at their point of use.
- Keep unknown local values and unreceived approvals null or explicitly unresolved.
- Separate resource consequences, cash-realisable budget changes, staffed capacity,
  NWAU and actual funding. Do not add these measures together.
- State the outcome clock, eligible cohort, denominator, missingness and uncertainty
  interpretation alongside results. Financial outputs do not establish safety.
- Keep reports reproducible and reviewable through source versions, seeds and hashes.
- Use plain language, explicit units and source links. Retain historical receipts
  as dated records and make current project status discoverable from the index.
- Use only synthetic fixtures in Git. The original workbook, publisher PDFs and
  private clinical, staffing and costing records remain outside this repository.
